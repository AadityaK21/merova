Open Merova Fit

One person, a few simple apps.

Merova is an independent app studio run by me, Aaditya Kumawat.

the story

I'm a B.Tech student at IIT Delhi. I design, build and ship every Merova app myself, from the screens to the servers.

Merova began with a fitness coaching app I built. When it became fully my own product, I wanted a name that could hold more than one app. That name is Merova, and the app is now Merova Fit.

Merova is the parent brand. Each app under it gets its own name, its own address and one clear job.

how I work

  • Small apps that each do one job.If an app tries to do everything, it gets hard to use. I'd rather make it do one thing well.
  • Plain language.In the app, on this site and in the policies. If something is unclear, tell me and I'll fix it.
  • Careful with your data.I collect what an app needs to work, and I don't sell it. The privacy policy has the details.
  • You can reach me.Emails to support@merova.app come straight to me.

the mark

The Merova logo is called Fold: two planes that overlap to make an M. The left one is solid. The right one sits behind it at 42%. It's simple on purpose, like the apps.

what's next

Merova Fit comes first, on the web at fit.merova.app and then on Android. More apps will follow when they're ready. No dates until they're real.
