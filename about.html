(Studio)

Independent by nature. Digital by design.

Merova is an independent digital studio run by me, Aaditya Kumawat. I design websites, build web apps, and make products of my own.

The story

I'm a B.Tech student at IIT Delhi. I design and build everything at Merova myself, from the screens to the servers.

Merova began with a fitness coaching app I built, which became Merova Fit. I wanted a name that could hold more than one app, and more than apps: websites, tools and experiments too. That name is Merova.

It's founder-led on purpose. No account managers, no hand-offs. The person who designs it is the person who builds it.

What I make

  • Websites.Designed and built from scratch, fast, and easy to keep up to date.
  • Web apps.Real products with accounts, data and payments, like Merova Fit and Company Hub.
  • Products of my own.Small apps that each do one everyday job well.
  • Experiments.Ideas I'm exploring, like The Fold. Always marked as experiments.

How I work

  • One clear job.Every project starts with the problem it solves and who it's for.
  • Design and code together.I design in the browser, so motion and detail are real from day one.
  • Careful with data.I collect what's needed and protect it. The privacy policy has the details.
  • You can reach me.Emails to hello@merova.app come straight to me.

The mark

The Merova logo is called Fold: two planes that overlap to make an M.

The left plane is solid. The right one sits behind it at 42%. Both are cut at 45°. Turn it around on the home page, or read about the experiment.
