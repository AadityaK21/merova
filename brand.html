Logo

Use the Ink version on light backgrounds and the Bone version on dark ones.

The mark is called Fold.

Two planes overlap to make an M. The left plane is solid. The right one sits behind it at 42%. Both are cut at 45°.

It's simple on purpose, like the apps.

Using the logo

do

  • Use the files as they are.
  • Put the Bone logo on Ink and the Ink logo on Paper or white.
  • Leave clear space around it.
  • Write the names as "Merova" and "Merova Fit".

don't

  • Stretch, rotate or redraw it.
  • Change its colours or the 42% plane.
  • Add shadows, outlines or gradients.
  • Use Sage for anything that isn't Merova Fit.
