02The mark

It's called Fold.

Two planes overlap to make an M. The left plane is solid. The right one sits behind it at 42%. Both are cut at 45°. It's simple on purpose.

05Rules

Using the logo.

Do

  • Use the files as they are.
  • Put the ivory logo on black and the black logo on ivory or white.
  • Leave clear space around it.
  • Write the names as "Merova" and "Merova Fit".

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or redraw it.
  • Change its colours or the 42% plane.
  • Add shadows, outlines or gradients.
  • Use vermilion as a background or a large fill.
