01A day in the app

Three screens, one day.

Real screens from the app, with demo data.

  1. 01Today

    Your whole day on one screen.

    A day score out of 100 sums up what you've logged: your workout, your calories and your water. Your next live class sits right at the top.

  2. 02Train

    Today's session, move by move.

    Every exercise with its sets and reps. Tick them off and watch the session fill up.

  3. 03Eat

    Food, counted without the fuss.

    Calories left, protein, carbs and fat at a glance, and each meal of your plan logged with one tap.

02Everything in it

What you can do.

  • Your daily plan

    Today's workout, exercise by exercise, with sets and reps. Tick each one off as you go.

  • Meals and calories

    Log what you eat and watch calories and macros add up through the day. Follow a meal plan if you have one.

  • Water

    Tap to add a glass and see the day fill up. Small habit, easy to keep.

  • Weight and measurements

    Log your weight and body measurements and see the trend over weeks, not just today.

  • Day score

    One number that sums up how today went, built from what you logged.

  • Goals, streaks and badges

    Daily, weekly and monthly goals, with streaks and badges that make showing up feel good.

  • Chat with your coach

    Ask questions about your plan and get answers from a real person, right in the app.

  • Book coaching calls

    Pick a time that suits you. You get a confirmation by email or WhatsApp.

Also: a shop with Razorpay checkout, reminders, and light and dark mode.

For coaches

  • ClientsSee each client's logs, progress and messages in one place.
  • PlansWrite workout and meal plans and update them as clients improve.
  • RevenueKeep track of plans sold, payments and shop orders.
  • BroadcastsSend one message to all your clients at once.

Who makes Merova Fit?

Merova, an independent digital studio in India. The person who builds the app also answers your support emails. More about the studio.

Can I use Merova Fit now?

Merova Fit runs on the web at fit.merova.app, and is coming to Android through Google Play. Join the list above to hear when it lands there.

How much does Merova Fit cost?

Plans and prices are shown inside Merova Fit before you pay anything.

Is my data safe?

Merova only collects what an app needs to work and never sells your data. Payments go through Razorpay, so card and UPI details never reach us. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 7 days of your first payment for a plan and you get your money back. See the refund policy.
