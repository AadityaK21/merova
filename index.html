03How I work

How I work

Every project starts with one clear job. Then I design it and build it end to end, from the first sketch to the server it runs on.

  1. 01

    Understand

    The problem and the people it's for, before any screens. One clear job.

  2. 02

    Design

    Type, layout and motion designed together, in the browser, so what you see is what ships.

  3. 03

    Build

    Fast, accessible and secure code, tested on real devices before it goes live.

  4. 04

    Look after it

    Launch isn't the end. I fix, measure and improve what's live.
