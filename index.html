07Questions

Questions.

Something else? Ask here.

What is Merova?

Merova is the parent brand for a small family of apps. Each app has its own name and does one everyday job. Merova Fit, a fitness coaching app, is the first.

Who makes Merova?

Aaditya Kumawat, on his own. He designs, builds and supports every Merova app. More about Merova.

Can I use Merova Fit now?

Merova Fit runs on the web at fit.merova.app, and is coming to Android through Google Play. Join the list above to hear when it lands there.

How much does Merova Fit cost?

Plans and prices are shown inside Merova Fit before you pay anything.

Is my data safe?

Merova only collects what an app needs to work and never sells your data. Payments go through Razorpay, so card and UPI details never reach us. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 7 days of your first payment for a plan and you get your money back. See the refund policy.

How do I delete my account?

From inside the app, or by emailing support@merova.app. Here’s exactly what gets deleted.

How do I get help?

Email support@merova.app. It goes straight to the person who builds the app.
