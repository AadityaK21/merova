04How it works

How a project works

Every project starts with one clear job. Then it's designed and built end to end, from the first sketch to the server it runs on.

  1. 01

    Talk

    You tell me what you're making and why. The enquiry form or a plain email is fine.

  2. 02

    Scope and quote

    We agree what's in and what isn't. You get a written quote and timeline before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    Designed in the browser and built in the open, with previews you can click through as it grows.

  4. 04

    Launch and look after

    I ship it, test it on real devices, and stay around to fix and improve it.
