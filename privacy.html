The short version

  • We collect what Merova Fit needs to work: your account details, the fitness data you log, your chats and bookings, and your orders.
  • We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use it for ads.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing support@merova.app.

Who we are

Merova is run by Aaditya Kumawat, a sole proprietor based in India ("Merova", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use:

  • this website, merova.app;
  • the Merova Fit web app at fit.merova.app; and
  • the Merova Fit Android app.

Together we call these "the services". For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Merova is the data fiduciary for the personal data described here.

We only collect what we need to run the services.

How we use your data

  • To run your account and the features you use, like plans, logging, chat, bookings and the shop.
  • To let your coach see your logs and messages so they can coach you.
  • To take payments, send receipts and process refunds.
  • To send service messages: sign-in codes by email, receipts, and booking confirmations by email or WhatsApp.
  • To keep the services secure, prevent fraud and fix bugs.
  • To understand, in total and not per person, which features are used, so we can improve them.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as keeping tax records.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We only send you marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and every such email has a way to unsubscribe.

Consent and legal basis

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you create an account and agree to this policy, and for other uses that Indian law allows, such as meeting legal obligations.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing support@merova.app or by deleting your account. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before, but some features will stop working, because we can't run them without the data.

We also share data:

  • with your coach, as described above;
  • with authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety; and
  • with a new owner, if Merova or an app is ever sold or transferred. We'd tell you first, and this policy would keep applying to your data.

Some providers store data on servers outside India. We only use well-known providers that protect data with strong security, and we transfer data only as Indian law allows.

Cookies and local storage

This website, merova.app, does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Merova Fit stores a sign-in token on your device so you stay signed in, and remembers settings like light or dark mode. These are needed for the app to work. If we ever add analytics, we'll update this policy first.

How long we keep data

  • Account and app data: for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days.
  • Order, payment and invoice records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require, which is generally up to 8 years.
  • Server and error logs: up to [90] days.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in backups for up to [30] days before it's overwritten.

Your rights

Under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we've shared it with;
  • correct, complete or update your data;
  • have your data erased;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to; and
  • raise a complaint with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email support@merova.app from the email address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days. To delete your account, follow the steps on Delete your account.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, sign-in codes, and access limited to the people and systems that need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that data will never be exposed. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age limit

The services are for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the services change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
