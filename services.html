01What I make

Four kinds of work.

Pick the closest one. Most projects mix a little of two.

  1. 01

    Website design and development

    A site designed from a blank page and built by hand. Fast on any phone, accessible, and easy to keep up to date. For a studio, a shop front, a launch or a personal brand.

    • Layout, type and art direction
    • Responsive build, no page builders
    • Hosting, domain and forms set up
    • Search basics and fast loading
    Start a project ↗ for website design and development
  2. 02

    Interactive and motion-led websites

    For when the site itself should be remembered. Scroll-driven storytelling, objects you can play with, considered sound and transitions, still quick and still usable with a keyboard.

    • Scroll and hover motion
    • Interactive pieces like The Fold
    • Optional UI sound
    • A calm version for reduced motion
    Start a project ↗ for interactive and motion-led websites
  3. 03

    Web applications and digital products

    Accounts, data, dashboards and payments, designed and built end to end. Merova Fit and Company Hub are both built this way.

    • Sign-in, roles and dashboards
    • Databases and APIs
    • Payment flows through providers like Razorpay
    • Deployment and monitoring
    Start a project ↗ for web applications and digital products
  4. 04

    Product design and ongoing improvements

    Already have a site or app? I can redesign the parts that confuse people, fix what's slow, and keep improving it after launch, by arrangement.

    • Design and UX review
    • Speed and accessibility fixes
    • Redesigns of key screens
    • Regular improvements, if agreed
    Start a project ↗ for product design and ongoing improvements

02How it works

Enquiry, scope, quote, then the work.

  1. 01

    Talk

    You tell me what you're making and why. The enquiry form or a plain email is fine.

  2. 02

    Scope and quote

    We agree what's in and what isn't. You get a written quote and timeline before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    Designed in the browser and built in the open, with previews you can click through as it grows.

  4. 04

    Launch and look after

    I ship it, test it on real devices, and stay around to fix and improve it.

Good to know

  • Pricing.Every project is quoted after we've talked it through. There are no fixed packages or public prices yet.
  • Payment.Only after we agree the scope and you accept a written quote. Payment terms are part of the quote.
  • The work so far.The projects on this site are self-initiated. Client case studies will be added as they ship, with permission.
  • Where.Based in India, working remotely. Email is the main channel, with calls when they help.
