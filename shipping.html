Digital plans and coaching

Plans, coaching and other digital services are delivered online in your Merova Fit account at fit.merova.app and in the Android app. Nothing is shipped.

  • Access starts as soon as your payment is confirmed, usually within a few minutes.
  • You get a receipt at the email address on your account.
  • If your plan isn't active within 24 hours of paying, email support@merova.app with your payment ID.
